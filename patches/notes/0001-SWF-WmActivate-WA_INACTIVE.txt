mcs/class/System.Windows.Forms/System.Windows.Forms/Form.cs: avoid infinite
activation recursion on Win9x when a modal dialog is shown.

Win9x delivers WM_ACTIVATE(WA_INACTIVE) synchronously to the (disabled) owner
while the dialog is being activated.  WmActivate re-activated the top modal
dialog regardless of wParam, producing:

    Form.Activate -> XplatUI.Activate -> WM_ACTIVATE(WA_INACTIVE) -> Form.Activate ...

until the stack overflowed (SEH c00000fd).  Only bounce activation back when
the disabled form is actually being activated.
