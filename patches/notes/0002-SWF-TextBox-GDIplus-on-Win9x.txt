mcs/class/System.Windows.Forms/System.Windows.Forms/TextBoxTextRenderer.cs:
use GDI+ text rendering for TextBox on Windows 9x/ME.

On Windows, TextBox draws its document through TextRenderer (GDI, i.e.
user32!DrawTextW via MSLU).  On the Win98 build this silently renders
nothing: the TextBox keeps its Text property and accepts typed input, but
the control's client area stays blank.  Fall back to Graphics.DrawString
(GDI+), which is what Mono already uses on Unix, when the OS major version
is below 5.
